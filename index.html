Diary and check-ins

Make room for the details that matter to you.

The app includes a structured SMER-style diary and local check-ins for mood, energy, and stress. These are tools for self-observation—not clinical monitoring.

Entries are stored in the app’s local data store. Optional features may use Firebase for analytics and diagnostics when enabled, and the app includes a Google Drive backup option initiated by the user. Read the privacy policy.

FAQ

Clear answers, without big promises.

Is CBT Cards a replacement for therapy?

No. CBT Cards is a general wellness and self-reflection tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and it does not replace a qualified professional.

Does the app work offline?

Cards and locally stored entries are available on-device. Some optional services, such as cloud backup, analytics, and diagnostics, require an internet connection.

Where is my diary data stored?

The app stores diary and check-in data locally using Hive. It also supports an optional user-initiated Google Drive backup. See the privacy policy for the confirmed details.
