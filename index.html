CBT-inspired tools for everyday reflection

CBT cards and a private journal for difficult moments.

CBT Cards combines short reflection prompts, guided tools, a structured CBT-style journal, and mood, stress, and energy check-ins in one mobile app.

Get it on Google Play Download on the App Store

CBT Cards is for general wellness and self-reflection. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or a substitute for professional care.

Everyday use

A practical tool when you need a little structure.

Stressful moments

Open a short card when a thought or feeling needs a slower, more deliberate response.

Thought journaling

Write down a situation, thoughts, emotions, body reactions, behaviour, and an alternative perspective in a structured entry.

Personal check-ins

Record mood, stress, and energy for your own reflection, without treating the result as a diagnosis or clinical score.

How it works

One small exercise at a time.

  1. 1. Choose a cardOpen a short card, metaphor, protocol, or guided tool that fits the moment.
  2. 2. Work through the promptPause to notice thoughts, feelings, behaviour, and what you may want to try next.
  3. 3. Write or check inWhen useful, add a journal entry or record mood, stress, or energy.
  4. 4. Review laterReturn to earlier entries and your own local patterns over time.

Journal and privacy

Make room for details that matter to you.

Journal and check-in content is stored locally in the app. Optional online services have separate, documented roles.

The app does not require an account for core cards, journal entries, or check-ins. Optional analytics and diagnostics can be controlled by an in-app consent setting, and Google Drive backup is initiated by the user. Read the privacy policy.

FAQ

Clear answers, without big promises.

Is CBT Cards a replacement for therapy?

No. CBT Cards is a general wellness and self-reflection tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and it does not replace a qualified professional.

Does the app work offline?

Cards and locally stored entries are available on-device. Some optional services, such as cloud backup, analytics, and diagnostics, require an internet connection.

Where is my journal data stored?

The app stores journal and check-in data locally using Hive. It also supports an optional user-initiated Google Drive backup. See the privacy policy for confirmed details.
