A small shift

Choose the moment before choosing the tool.

The library is organized around concrete situations: looping worry, repeated checking, avoidance, perfectionistic polishing, and harsh self-evaluation.

When there is a clear fit, the Practice Finder points to one reviewed practice with best-use notes, exclusions, evidence provenance, and a tiny action. When there is no safe fit, it returns no_match instead of inventing advice.
