About CBT Cards

Product identity, editorial principles, localization boundaries, and the public source of truth for CBT Cards.

What CBT Cards is

CBT Cards is a mobile app published by MetalHatsCats. It provides short CBT-inspired reflection cards, guided tools, a structured journal, and personal check-ins for mood, stress, and energy.

The app is designed for general wellness and self-reflection. It is not a medical device, a diagnostic service, emergency support, or a replacement for psychotherapy or other professional care.

How product information is maintained

This website is the canonical public documentation for CBT Cards. Feature, privacy, data-handling, and support claims should match the current application source and store listings. When the app changes, the relevant public documentation should be reviewed with the release.

Editorial approach

  • Use plain language and avoid claims that CBT Cards can diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent a condition.
  • Distinguish established CBT concepts from the way CBT Cards implements a reflection tool.
  • Link to primary or authoritative sources when a learning page explains a CBT concept.
  • Say when a product detail is not publicly confirmed rather than filling the gap with an assumption.
  • Keep safety and privacy limitations visible where they matter.

Health-adjacent learning pages, worksheets, and curated toolkit cards are tracked under a public editorial review and freshness policy. The machine-readable review registry records when each covered resource was last checked and when the next project review is due.

Website localization

The public website currently publishes English at the root URL. The website localization policy and locale registry describe website locales only; they are not a source for Android or iOS language support.

For researchers, developers, and agents

Public product facts and learning resources are available as ordinary web pages and through machine-readable resources. Start with For agents, llms.txt, the JSON catalog, or the JSON Schema manifest.

Contact and support

For practical help, privacy questions, or current support options, use the support page. Do not send private journal contents or other sensitive personal information when reporting an issue.
