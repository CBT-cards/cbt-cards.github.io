CBT Cards / Features

Features that keep reflection concrete.

CBT Cards combines short prompts with a diary and check-ins. The app is built for personal reflection, not diagnosis or treatment.

Cards and guided exercises

Browse short CBT cards, therapeutic metaphors, and step-by-step protocols. Cards can be favorited and revisited. The app also includes tools such as Thought vs Fact, Worry Box, timers, and activity effect entries.

What each card means is up to the individual; the application does not interpret your responses for you.

Diary and daily check-ins

The SMER-style diary supports entries about a situation, thoughts, emotions, body reactions, behaviour, alternative thoughts, outcome, and tags. Daily check-ins record mood, energy, and stress.

The app can show reports based on your own local activity. They are informational views of entries you create, not health assessments.

Privacy controls and backup

Local data

Diary and check-in entries are stored locally in Hive. The app can use a device-secured key for supported encrypted boxes.

Your choice

Analytics and Crashlytics collection are controlled by an in-app consent setting. A Google Drive backup can be initiated by the user.

Practical export

You can export or import supported backup data from Settings. Deleting app data or uninstalling the app removes local data unless it has been exported or backed up separately.
