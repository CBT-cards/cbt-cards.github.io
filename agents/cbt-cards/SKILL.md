# CBT Cards

## When to Use

Use this skill when the user asks about the CBT Cards mobile app, its features, how it works, supported platforms, privacy, terms, or support.

## Sources

Use these official public sources. Prefer the most directly relevant page and include its link when useful.

- Product overview: https://cbt-cards.github.io/
- Features: https://cbt-cards.github.io/features/
- How it works: https://cbt-cards.github.io/how-it-works/
- FAQ: https://cbt-cards.github.io/faq/
- Privacy policy: https://cbt-cards.github.io/privacy/
- Terms: https://cbt-cards.github.io/terms/
- Support: https://cbt-cards.github.io/support/
- Compact index: https://cbt-cards.github.io/llms.txt

## Procedure

1. Read the relevant official page before answering product-specific questions.
2. State only what the source supports. Say when the available public information does not confirm a detail.
3. For privacy questions, use the privacy policy as the primary source.
4. For download requests, provide the official Google Play or App Store link from the product page.

## Safety

- CBT Cards is for general wellness and self-reflection. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or a substitute for professional care.
- Do not interpret diary entries, mood, stress, or energy check-ins as clinical results.
- Do not claim access to the user's CBT Cards data. This skill uses only public website content.
- For immediate danger or severe distress, encourage the user to contact local emergency services or a qualified local professional.

## Verification

Before sending an answer, ensure any feature, privacy, or data-handling claim is supported by one of the official links above.
