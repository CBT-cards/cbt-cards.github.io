# CBT Cards is now part of Vedokrok

CBT Cards is now part of [Vedokrok](https://vedokrok.com) — a substantially broader practical knowledge system built by the same publisher, [MetalHatsCats](https://github.com/metalhatscats).

## Project history

**Experiment.** CBT Cards started as a mobile reflection app for general wellness and self-reflection. The public project asked a bigger question: can the useful core of an app outlive the app itself? The answer was to rebuild it as an inspectable public library — plain HTML and machine-readable data with stable IDs, canonical URLs, evidence notes, safety boundaries, review dates, and checks that fail when the project's own claims drift.

**Lessons.** Two conclusions proved durable:

1. Small, concrete, actionable techniques are the kind of practical knowledge people actually use. Long essays inform; compact techniques get used.
2. Trust has to be structural. The reviewed practice layer stays separate from raw source material; the Practice Finder returns `no_match` rather than inventing advice; publication status, editorial freshness, and safety exclusions are recorded as data, not implied by prose.

**Consolidation.** CBT Cards explored one domain — CBT-informed reflection — and took it as far as a focused project should go: 11 reviewed owned practices, 42 owned editorial modules, a pinned 115-record source toolkit with a record-complete audit, browser-local worksheets, evaluation tooling, and versioned Agent Skills.

**Vedokrok.** The strongest ideas and content from that work are now part of Vedokrok, which applies the same practical principle across many areas of life and work: techniques, mental models, decision tools, frameworks, checklists, protocols, exercises, and communication and learning methods, with evidence, collections, and future AI access. Before: a focused project around one area of practical knowledge. Now: Vedokrok connects thousands of practical ideas, techniques, frameworks and tools across many areas of life and work.

## What this means

- **The site stays up.** `https://cbt-cards.github.io/` remains online as a historical project and a useful standalone resource.
- **No redirects.** Existing URLs, canonicals, and bookmarks keep working; no page is removed or repointed.
- **Content is preserved.** Reviewed practices, worksheets, learning guides, toolkit audit, datasets, schemas, and the Agent Skill history remain available under their existing terms (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0).
- **Publisher is unchanged.** CBT Cards remains published and maintained by MetalHatsCats. This is a consolidation of the publisher's own projects, not a sale or a change of ownership.
- **Safety boundary is unchanged.** The material stays general wellness and self-reflection content; it does not diagnose, treat, or replace professional care.
- **Active development continues at Vedokrok.** New practical-knowledge work happens in the Vedokrok system rather than as new CBT Cards releases.
