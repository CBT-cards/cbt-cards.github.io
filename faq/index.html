Frequently asked questions

What is CBT Cards?

CBT Cards is a mobile app with short CBT-inspired cards, guided protocols, therapeutic metaphors, a structured diary, and mood, stress, and energy check-ins.

Is CBT Cards a replacement for therapy?

No. It is a general wellness and self-reflection tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Does the app work offline?

Cards and locally stored entries are available on-device. Optional services, such as cloud backup, analytics, and diagnostics, need internet access.

Where is my diary data stored?

The app stores diary and check-in data locally using Hive. A user-initiated Google Drive backup option is also available. Read the privacy policy for confirmed details.

Is the app free?

Store availability and pricing are controlled by Google Play and the App Store. Check the relevant listing for current information.

Is it available for Android and iOS?

Yes. Use the official Google Play or App Store listing.
