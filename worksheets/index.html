Privacy by construction

These are static web worksheets. There is no submit button or CBT Cards account behind them. Text you type into a worksheet stays in the current browser page and disappears when the page is refreshed or closed unless you independently print or save it.

Educational scope

The worksheets are general self-reflection resources. They do not diagnose a condition, score your answers, decide whether a thought is true, or tell you which treatment is appropriate.

Machine-readable worksheet fields are also available in data/worksheets.json for accessibility tools and agent integrations.
