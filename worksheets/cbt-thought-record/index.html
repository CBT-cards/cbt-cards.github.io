CBT thought record worksheet

Seven prompts for slowing down one difficult situation and examining what you thought, felt, and noticed before and after reviewing the evidence.

Private browser worksheet: there is no submit button. What you type stays in this page and is not sent to CBT Cards. Refreshing or closing the page clears it.

Print or save as PDF: use the button when you want a blank or completed copy.

Entries stay in this browser page only and clear when the page is refreshed or closed.

Read the step-by-step thought record guide →

1

Situation

What happened? Describe one specific situation using concrete details: who, what, when, and where.

2

Feelings at first

What emotions did you notice at the time? Use your own words; a rating is optional, not a score.

3

Thoughts

What thoughts, meanings, predictions, or images went through your mind?

4

Evidence that supports the thought

What directly known information supports the interpretation? Try to distinguish facts from assumptions.

5

Evidence that does not support the thought

What facts, context, past experience, or alternative information complicate or contradict the interpretation?

6

Alternative view

Considering both sides, is there a more realistic, neutral, or balanced way to describe the situation? This does not have to be positive.

7

Feelings now

After reviewing the situation, what do you notice now? It is also okay if your feelings did not change.

Source and limits

This worksheet follows the seven-prompt thought-record structure described by NHS Every Mind Matters. CBT Cards has paraphrased the prompts for a neutral, printable browser format. Read the NHS thought-record guide.

This is an educational self-reflection worksheet. It is not a diagnostic test, treatment plan, or substitute for individualized professional care.
