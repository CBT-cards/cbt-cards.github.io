How to use these pages

Use a guide to understand an exercise before trying it in the app or worksheet, or as a public reference when you need the underlying idea rather than a product description. Each guide names its sources and separates general CBT information from CBT Cards-specific implementation.

Clinical boundary

CBT is a form of talking therapy and is commonly delivered with a trained therapist. Self-reflection exercises and worksheets can be useful educational tools, but they do not determine whether CBT or any other treatment is right for a particular person.
