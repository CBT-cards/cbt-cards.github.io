CBT Cards / Worry time

Worry time

A way to record a worry without giving it the rest of the day.

What is worry time?

Worry time is a structured technique for setting aside a short, planned period to review worries rather than repeatedly engaging with them whenever they appear. One practical version is to write the worry down, remind yourself that you have a time to return to it, and redirect attention to the present task until that review period.

A simple sequence

  1. Capture the worry: write it in one clear sentence.
  2. Park it: decide that you will review it during a planned worry period rather than solve it immediately.
  3. Return to the present: bring attention back to what you were doing.
  4. Review later: ask whether the worry points to a practical problem you can act on or a hypothetical concern outside your immediate control.
  5. Make a plan where possible: for a solvable problem, choose a concrete next action and when you will take it.

How the CBT Cards Worry Box relates

CBT Cards includes a Worry Box tool. Its role is to give a worry a defined place instead of requiring you to keep rehearsing it mentally. The tool does not decide whether a worry is realistic, diagnose anxiety, or tell you what action is clinically appropriate.

When this is not enough

Worry is a normal human experience, but a self-reflection exercise is not a substitute for individualized support. If worry is persistent, overwhelming, or interfering substantially with daily life, consider discussing it with an appropriate qualified professional.
