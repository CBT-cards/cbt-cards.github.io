CBT Cards / Partnerships

Build useful public work with clear boundaries.

CBT Cards is open to focused collaboration where the audience, responsibility, evidence, rights, and maintenance path are explicit.

Where collaboration can help

Translation review

Native-language review, terminology decisions, safety wording, localized navigation, and publication checks for Russian, German, and future locales.

Accessibility

Keyboard and screen-reader audits, plain-language review, cognitive accessibility, print usability, and translation resilience.

Research and education

Carefully scoped educational use, evidence review, evaluation design, or curriculum links that do not overstate clinical validation.

AI and data integration

Agent Skills, public RAG, schemas, stable IDs, review freshness, provenance, and evaluation tooling that preserve avoid_when and no_match.

Responsible distribution

Curated resource lists, accessibility directories, public-interest tools, or editorial references that link to canonical reviewed pages.

Licensing conversation

Discuss uses that fall outside the current public license before assuming commercial or permissive rights.

What CBT Cards can provide

  • Canonical reviewed practice pages and machine-readable practice records.
  • Public schemas, changelog, review status, evidence provenance, and a pinned source toolkit.
  • A versioned Agent Skill and dependency-free reference client.
  • Browser-local worksheets and original paper-collage visual assets.
  • Clear separation between public resources and private app reflection data.

What a proposal should include

  1. Your organization or project and a public reference URL.
  2. The audience and concrete outcome.
  3. The resource, language, data, or integration you want to use.
  4. Who will review accuracy, accessibility, safety wording, and ongoing maintenance.
  5. Whether the use is commercial or non-commercial and how attribution will appear.
  6. A realistic timeline and the person responsible for follow-through.
No automatic endorsement or clinical claim. A link, data integration, translation draft, or research discussion does not make an organization an approved CBT Cards partner and does not establish clinical efficacy.
