# Semantic recommendation evaluation protocol v1

The deterministic route benchmark and this protocol measure different things. Report separately: situation/mechanism fit, practice appropriateness, safety-exclusion preservation, no diagnosis/treatment overclaim, evidence fidelity, publication-boundary fidelity, micro-action fidelity, no-match behavior, locale/publication behavior, and canonical citation correctness.

The dataset contains at least 40 separately authored situations. Deterministic checks may verify IDs, URLs, boundary flags and prohibited records. Human semantic review is the authority for contextual appropriateness. An LLM judge, if ever added, must remain inspectable and must not be the sole authority for safety correctness.

Expected judgments remain hidden from model generation. These evals do not measure clinical outcomes or prove therapeutic efficacy.
