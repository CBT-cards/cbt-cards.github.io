CBT Cards / How it works

Start with the moment in front of you.

The app is intended for short personal reflection. You decide what to write and what to take from it.

1

Choose a card

Open a CBT card, protocol, metaphor, or tool from the app.

2

Complete the prompt

Read the exercise and consider the question in your own words and context.

3

Write or check in

When it is useful, add a diary entry or record mood, stress, and energy.

4

Review later

Return to your favorites, diary entries, and local reports to notice patterns in what you recorded.

5

Choose your settings

In Settings, you can manage reminders, a PIN lock, backups, analytics consent, and language preferences.

6

Get support when needed

The app is not emergency support. If you are in immediate danger or severe distress, contact local emergency services or a qualified local professional.
