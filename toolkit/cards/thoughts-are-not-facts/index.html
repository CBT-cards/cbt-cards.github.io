Thoughts ≠ Facts

A short reminder that a thought can be important and emotionally convincing without automatically being a complete description of reality.

Try the card

  1. Write one thought that has been on your mind.
  2. Separate what you directly know from what you are interpreting, predicting, or assuming.
  3. Ask what evidence supports the interpretation.
  4. Ask what evidence or context might complicate it.

The aim is not to label the thought “wrong.” It is to make room for evidence and alternative explanations before acting on it.
