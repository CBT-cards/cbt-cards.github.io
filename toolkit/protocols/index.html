Why the full protocols are not mirrored here yet

Protocol titles can look like treatment claims even when the source is intended as general product content. The source records do not include per-record clinical-review metadata, so CBT Cards does not automatically generate standalone health-guidance pages from them.

Before a protocol becomes a public standalone page, its terminology, scope, steps, references, duplicate IDs/titles, and wellness boundary should be reviewed explicitly.

Known source-quality note

protocol-1 and protocol-8 currently share the title “Burnout Recovery Protocol.” This is recorded in data/toolkit-source.json so agents and future generators do not silently treat the title as a unique identifier.

The index itself is not medical advice and does not recommend any protocol for an individual user.
