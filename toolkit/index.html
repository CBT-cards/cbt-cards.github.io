Machine-readable source

The complete corpus remains available as NDJSON from the public MetalHatsCats datasets repository. This site records the pinned source commit, blob SHA, counts, licensing, and known quality notes in data/toolkit-source.json.

Agents should treat record IDs as source identifiers, preserve attribution, and avoid turning a short prompt into diagnosis or individualized treatment advice.

Editorial boundary

The source dataset has no per-record clinical-review metadata. It also contains CBT-adjacent terminology in addition to core CBT concepts. Appearance in the corpus therefore means “public toolkit content,” not “clinically validated intervention.”
