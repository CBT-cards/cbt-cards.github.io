Do not include sensitive information. Do not send diary entries, check-in text, diagnoses, medical details, passwords, backup files, access tokens, or other private content. GitHub issues are public. Use LinkedIn for a non-public first contact, while still keeping sensitive reflection content out of the message.

CBT Cards does not publish a response-time guarantee. If a message concerns immediate danger or severe distress, this contact page is not emergency support; use appropriate local emergency or qualified professional help.
